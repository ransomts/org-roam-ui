#+title: Special Blocks Demo

This note demonstrates center, verse, and example block rendering
in the org-roam-ui preview pane.

* Center Block

#+begin_center
This text is centered.

It can span multiple paragraphs, and all of them
will be centered within the preview pane.
#+end_center

* Verse Block

#+begin_verse
Two roads diverged in a yellow wood,
And sorry I could not travel both
And be one traveler, long I stood
And looked down one as far as I could
To where it bent in the undergrowth;

  — Robert Frost, /The Road Not Taken/
#+end_verse
